Support · Privacy

Privacy policy

QuestDaily News is operated by Manohar Gadkari. Contact manohargadkari@gmail.com with privacy questions.

On your device

No account is required. Your country, city, feed language, topic rules, bookmarks, notes, questions, reading status, collections, and cached headlines are stored on your device. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK and does not request GPS location, contacts, camera, or microphone access. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Network requests

Refreshing connects to Global Voices for your selected language. Global Voices receives your IP address and basic connection information. City and country are used only for matching on your device and are not sent in feed requests. We do not operate a server receiving preferences, research, bookmarks, or reading history. Feed requests use an ephemeral network session without a persistent app cookie cache.

Publisher websites

Opening an article or publisher website connects to that publisher and may involve redirects. Sites can use cookies, advertising, subscriptions, and analytics under their own privacy policies. QuestDaily News does not control these practices.

Sharing your research

Export personal briefing opens the system share sheet. The briefing contains the displayed headlines, original links, credits, read status, and your personal notes and questions. Choosing a destination sends this content there under that destination's policies. Sharing is initiated by you.

Retention and deletion

During refresh cleanup, unsaved headlines expire after 14 days or when the cache exceeds 2,000 items. Stories with bookmarks, notes, questions, or collection membership remain. Clear cached headlines preserves these stories. Deleting a collection keeps its stories bookmarked. Reset all app data removes all local stories, research, collections, topic rules, and preferences. These actions do not erase previously shared briefings, third-party website records, or existing device backups.
