Privacy policy

QuestDaily News is operated by Manohar Gadkari. Contact manohargadkari@gmail.com with privacy questions.

What stays on your device

No account is required. Country, language, city, topic rules, bookmarks, and cached headlines are stored on your device. Eligible text-only Global Voices stories may also be cached for offline reading; images, video, and audio are not republished. The app does not request GPS, camera, microphone, or contacts access. Notes, questions, collections, and reading status stay on the device unless you choose to share a briefing.

Requests to news services

Refreshing news connects to the Global Voices website for your selected language, which receives your IP address and normal connection information. Your city and country stay on your device and are used only to match headlines and categories. The developer does not operate a server collecting preferences, bookmarks, or reading history. Feed requests use an ephemeral network session without a persistent app cookie cache.

Publisher websites

Opening stories and publisher links connects to third-party sites and may involve redirects. These sites may use cookies, analytics, advertising, and subscription services under their own policies. QuestDaily News does not control those practices.

Advertising

The app displays labeled banner ads from Google AdMob after Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent checks permit ad requests. iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) controls whether an advertising identifier may be used for personalized ads. If tracking is not authorized, ad requests are configured for non-personalized ads. Google may process the device’s IP address and approximate region, device or advertising identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostic information, and performance data to deliver, limit, and measure ads. Google may use mobile advertising identifiers for frequency capping and aggregated ad reporting even when ads are non-personalized. Consent choices are available in the app’s Settings when required; ATT permission can be changed in iOS Settings. Google’s privacy information is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Retention and choices

Unbookmarked cached headlines and eligible article text are removed during successful refresh cleanup after 14 days or when the cache exceeds 2,000 items. Stories saved to your notebook remain until removed or app data is reset. Settings lets you clear the headline cache or reset all app data. Device backups may contain app data according to your Apple settings. Removing data from the app does not remove third-party website records or existing device backups.

Changes

Changes to this policy will appear here with a revised date. Contact the support email above about access, deletion, or privacy concerns.
