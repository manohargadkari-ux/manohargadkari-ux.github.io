Support · Privacy

Support

Your personal news notebook. For help, email manohargadkari@gmail.com with your app version, device model, and a description of the issue. Please do not send passwords or sensitive information.

Save and research a story

Open a headline in Discover. In Story workspace, enable Keep in notebook, write notes or questions, and tap Save. Back also saves changes before returning. Save before switching tabs.

Collections and search

In Notebook, create a collection. Open a saved story, enable the collection in its workspace, and save. Choose All collections if the new collection is empty. Pull down the Notebook list to reveal search and search headlines, notes, or questions. Turn off Unread only if a read story is hidden.

Daily review and export

Daily review shows unread saved stories. Mark a story read in its workspace and save to remove it from the queue. Export personal briefing shares the stories currently shown and their credits, links, personal notes, and questions.

Offline use and refresh

Saved headlines and research can be used offline after saving. Feed refresh and full publisher articles require an internet connection. If no stories appear, try World, another supported language, or less restrictive topic rules. Local and National coverage is limited to matching available Global Voices headlines and categories. The interface is English.

Privacy and storage

Settings contains the privacy policy, clear-cache action, and Reset all app data. Cache clearing preserves research. Reset removes all local research and preferences. Publisher websites have their own ads, subscriptions, and privacy practices.
